Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026.

This policy explains what happens to information when you use this site, New Vegas Recompiled (the "site").

Your game files

The game runs from a folder on your computer that you choose. The browser reads those files locally; the game page does not upload them to us.

What stays on your device

The site stores things in your browser's storage: your saves and the game's settings, the link to your game folder if you pressed "Choose and remember folder", the Auto quality choice, and your chat name and chat preferences. You can remove them by clearing the site's data in your browser.

Chat

The public chat sends the name you pick (or "Anonymous") and the messages you write to our chat server, which shows them to everyone in the room. The server keeps only the most recent messages so that people who arrive can see them. Your country, worked out from your connection, is shown as a flag next to your messages; your IP address is not shown to other players. Anything you write in chat is public: do not put personal information in it. Messages can be translated into other languages; to do that the text of a message may be sent to Google's translation service (or, where your browser offers it, translated on your device).

Problem reports

If you press "Report a problem" and send a report, we receive what you typed, any contact detail you chose to give, and technical information to find the fault: the game's log, your browser and device type, graphics card information, performance figures, and optionally a screenshot and your latest save. Reports are used only to fix problems and are kept only as long as that needs.

Hosting and logs

The site is delivered through Cloudflare, which processes requests (including your IP address) to serve the pages and protect the site, and may keep standard security and traffic logs. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Advertising and cookies

Some pages of the site (the information pages, not the game or the chat) show advertising from Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show and measure ads, and use advertising cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you are asked for your consent to advertising cookies and can change your choice at any time.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and the game is rated for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may ask what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. We keep very little, and most of it is on your own device, where you can remove it yourself. For anything else, contact us.

Changes

We will change this page when the site changes, and update the date above.
